About Dubai Pet Guardian

Dedicated to the health, happiness, and safety of your beloved pets across the UAE.

Our Story

Founded in the heart of Dubai, Dubai Pet Guardian was born from a simple belief: pets are family, and they deserve the highest standard of care when their owners are away.


What started as a small, passionate team of animal lovers has grown into the UAE's most trusted network of professional pet sitters, dog walkers, and boarding specialists. We understand the unique needs of pets in the region, from navigating the summer heat during walks to providing serene, stress-free environments for anxious cats.
